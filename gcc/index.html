Common questions

Do you work with clients in Saudi Arabia and the rest of the GCC?

Yes. Most work is remote, with on-site visits for workshops, go-lives, and board meetings. Calls are scheduled in the Gulf afternoon, which is the U.S. Eastern morning.

Can you work in Arabic?

Yes. Working sessions and meetings can be in Arabic or English, so finance teams, owners, and executives can each work in the language they prefer.

Do you handle VAT, zakat, or ZATCA e-invoicing compliance?

I design the finance processes and systems, and I coordinate with your local tax advisor, auditor, and ERP partner, who are responsible for tax advice and compliance certification. I don’t provide tax or legal advice.

Which systems do you work with in the region?

NetSuite, Oracle, and Microsoft Dynamics on the transaction side, and Power BI, Tableau, SQL, and Snowflake for reporting, including multi-entity and multi-currency setups.
