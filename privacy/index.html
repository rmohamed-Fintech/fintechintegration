Privacy policy

How Fintech Integrations LLC handles information from visitors to fintechintegration.com. Effective November 18, 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Fintech Integrations LLC, a Michigan advisory practice led by Rafat Mohamed. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” mean Fintech Integrations LLC. Questions about this policy can be sent to rmohamed@fintechintegration.com.

Information we collect

Information you send us

If you submit the engagement inquiry form or email us, we receive the information you provide: your name, title, email address, phone number, company details, and whatever you choose to share about your company, its systems, or its finances. Form submissions are processed by Formspree, which delivers them to our email. The site has no user accounts.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors use the site. It uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as the pages you view, how long you stay, how you arrived at the site, your browser and device type, and your approximate location based on your IP address. We use this information in aggregate and do not use it to identify you personally.

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and browser information.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log the IP addresses of visitors for security and to operate its service.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry and, if we work together, to deliver and invoice our services.
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve the site.
  • To keep the site secure and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising based on your activity on other sites.

Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that run this site, its inquiry form, and our email, namely Google, GitHub, and Formspree, which process it under their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or as part of a sale or reorganization of the business. Information you send about your company in confidence is handled as confidential business information.

Visitors outside the United States

We are based in the United States, and our service providers process information in the United States and other countries. If you contact us from outside the United States, including from Saudi Arabia or another GCC country, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. You can ask us about, correct, or delete your information as described below.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics on all websites with Google’s opt-out browser add-on.
  • You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing rmohamed@fintechintegration.com. We may keep information we are required to retain for legal, tax, or accounting purposes.

The site does not currently respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals.

How long we keep information

We keep emails and engagement records for as long as needed to respond to you, provide services, and meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. Google Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings in our Google Analytics account.

Security

The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please do not email highly sensitive information such as account numbers or passwords.

Children

This site is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date at the top.
