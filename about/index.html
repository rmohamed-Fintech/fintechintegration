About Rafat Mohamed

Finance, operations, and technology executive with 14+ years inside cold-chain logistics, healthcare insurance, consumer products, and multi-unit retail. I started Fintech Integration to bring that operator experience to growing companies.

Why I do this work

Growing companies tend to hit the same wall. The business gets more complex faster than its systems and reporting, and leadership ends up making growth decisions on numbers nobody fully trusts. I’ve been the person responsible for fixing that from the inside, and I’ve seen what it costs when it isn’t fixed.

Clients get one senior advisor who does the work, not a team that learns on their account. I earn trust with the people closest to the work first, because they know where the numbers break.
