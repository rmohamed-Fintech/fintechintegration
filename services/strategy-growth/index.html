Business strategy and growth planning

Growth decisions are capital decisions. I help leadership teams decide where to grow, what to fix, and what to stop, using unit economics they trust and a plan the finance function can actually track.

Questions this answers

  • Which units actually make money?After rent, labor, royalties, and a fair share of overhead, by location, product line, or customer.
  • Where should the next dollar go?New locations, an acquisition, a system, or paying down debt, compared on the same return basis.
  • What does a new unit need to return?The hurdle a site or deal has to clear, and how long until it pays back.
  • Can we fund the plan?What has to be true for next year’s growth to work, and what happens to cash if it doesn’t.
  • Are we ready for outside capital?Whether the numbers will hold up for a lender, an investor, or a buyer.

Common questions

Is this a fractional CFO service?

It can be. Many clients start with a defined strategy project and continue with fractional leadership for a set number of days a month.

How is this different from my accountant?

Your accountant records what happened and handles tax and compliance. I focus on what to do next. I work alongside your accountant, not instead of them.

Are you a CPA?

No. I don’t prepare taxes, issue audit opinions, or sign financial statements. My background is corporate finance, FP&A, cost accounting, and operations.

Do you work with private equity-backed companies?

Yes. The planning, tracking, and reporting are built to the standard a sponsor or lender expects.
