Profitability and EBITDA improvement

Revenue growth that doesn’t reach EBITDA is the most common problem in a growing company. I find where the margin goes, size each fix from your own data, and help you deliver it.

Where the margin usually goes

  • Pricing that lags costPrices set once and never revisited, discounts nobody tracks, and products sold below their real cost to serve.
  • Unprofitable customers, products, or locationsHidden inside averages until cost is allocated properly and each one has its own P&L.
  • Labor scheduled to habit, not demandHours that don’t follow sales, and overtime that becomes permanent.
  • Vendor and software spend nobody ownsAuto-renewing contracts, duplicate tools, and terms that never kept pace with your volume.
  • Manual finance workSlow closes and spreadsheet reconciliations that cost salary and delay every decision.

What you get

An EBITDA bridgeFrom today’s EBITDA to the target, with each move sized from your data and ranked by value and effort.
Profitability by unitA P&L by location, product line, or customer, with overhead allocated in a way your team agrees is fair.
A 90-day action planOwners, dates, and the monthly measure for each fix, so the bridge becomes accountability, not a slide.
Monthly trackingA short dashboard comparing results to the plan, built on numbers that reconcile to the books.

At Lineage Logistics, I negotiated multi-year software agreements that saved $20M+ and remediated cost accounting before the 2024 IPO.

Common questions

How is this different from a cost-cutting exercise?

Cuts are one lever of several. Pricing, mix, and exiting unprofitable work often add more EBITDA than cuts, without weakening the parts of the business that drive growth.

Do you work with private equity-backed companies?

Yes. The bridge and monthly tracking are built to the standard a sponsor or lender expects.

Are you a CPA firm?

No. I don’t prepare taxes or perform audits. I work alongside your accountant on the operating and investment decisions the numbers should support.
