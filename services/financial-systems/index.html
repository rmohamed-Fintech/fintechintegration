Financial systems integration and clarity

Your ERP, POS, payroll, and bank each hold part of the truth. I connect them so the books close fast, every unit has a P&L you can defend, and leadership stops arguing about whose number is right.

Signs you need this

  • Month-end close takes weeksBecause someone ties reports to the books by hand.
  • Nobody trusts the reportsDifferent systems give different answers, and every meeting starts with reconciling them.
  • You’ve outgrown your accounting softwareInventory lives in spreadsheets, and new locations or entities are added manually.
  • An implementation is off trackThe go-live date keeps moving, or the business works around the new system.
  • An acquisition brought another set of systemsAnd the combined numbers still don’t add up.

Common questions

Which ERP do you recommend?

It depends on how you operate. NetSuite fits many growing multi-location companies, but the answer comes from your requirements, your integrations, and total cost over five years.

Do you resell software or take vendor commissions?

My recommendation is based on your requirements. If a referral arrangement with any vendor ever applies to your project, I’ll tell you before you decide.

Can you take over an implementation that’s off track?

Yes. I assess scope, data quality, and partner performance, then reset the plan around what the business needs at go-live.

Do you configure systems yourself?

I lead the program and direct the implementation partners and developers who configure and build, and I specify and check what they deliver.

Get to one set of numbers.

Tell me which systems you run and where the numbers break. I’ll tell you what it would take to fix it.
