How engagements work

Start small, decide with evidence. Many clients stop after the diagnostic with a clear plan.

A fixed-fee review of your systems, reporting, and unit economics. You get the EBITDA bridge, the fixes in priority order, and what each is worth.

Project

A defined deliverable: an ERP selection or rescue, a reporting rebuild, a profitability model, or an acquisition integration. I manage the partners and own the outcome.
