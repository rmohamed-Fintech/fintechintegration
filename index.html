How engagements work

Most clients start with a short diagnostic. Some stop there with a clear plan. Others continue into a project or ongoing support.

Diagnostic

A fixed-scope review of your systems, reporting, and unit economics. You get a written assessment of what's broken, what it costs you, and the order to fix it in.

Project

A defined deliverable: a POS conversion, an ERP selection, a reporting rebuild, or a store-level profitability model. I manage the vendors and partners and own the outcome.

Fractional support

Ongoing finance and systems leadership for a set number of days per month, for companies that need senior judgment but not a full-time executive yet.

Common questions

Do you only work with restaurants?

No. Restaurant and franchise brands are my focus because I've run one, but the same problems show up in any business growing across locations, product lines, or acquisitions. I've done this work in consumer goods, cold-chain logistics, and healthcare.

Which systems do you work with?

NetSuite, Toast POS, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, and Sage 100 on the transaction side, and Power BI, Tableau, SQL, and Snowflake on the reporting side. If you're on something else, the accounting and data problems are usually the same.

Are you a CPA or an accounting firm?

No. I don't prepare taxes or perform audits. I work alongside your accountant, focusing on systems, reporting, and the operating and investment decisions the numbers should support.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I'm based in metro Detroit and work with clients across the U.S. On-site time is part of most projects, especially store rollouts and go-lives.

Do you write code or configure systems yourself?

I lead implementations and direct the partners and developers who configure and build. I've managed developer teams and know enough about the data to specify exactly what needs to be built and to catch it when it's wrong.

Tell me what isn't adding up

Send a few lines on your business, your locations, your systems, and what's breaking. I'll tell you honestly whether I can help.
